There is something lovely about an empty notebook. No elaborate plan, no perfect first sentence. Just a place to begin.

This is a sample post, holding the door open for the real words to come. Some might be about making things for the web. Others might be notes from a walk, a book, or a question I have been turning over.

## A place to put things

I like the idea of a website that can grow slowly. A page here, a thought there. Something that feels lived in, rather than finished.

> Small things, given a little attention, have a way of becoming something.

A few things I might leave here:

- Notes on things I am learning.
- Little projects and the stories behind them.
- Links I would like to find again.
