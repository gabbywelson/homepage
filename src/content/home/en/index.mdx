I’m a <Accent icon="engineer">software engineer</Accent>, cat mom to Nori and Kimchi, collector of hobbies, and a curious lifelong learner. I live in San Francisco, but New England will always feel like home.

These days, I’m helping people find meaningful work training next-gen AI models as a founding engineer at [Handshake AI](https://joinhandshake.com/ai), tinkering on some (still top-secret) side projects, and finding time to [tend to my digital garden](/garden/).

Before Handshake, I grew from an engineering apprentice into a senior software engineer at [LinkedIn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn). I learned to code at [Hack Reactor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hack_Reactor), where I later taught as a Software Engineer in Residence. Some of the work I’m proudest of has been making software more accessible and helping other engineers from nontraditional backgrounds find their footing.

My path into tech had a few side quests. After studying political science and leadership studies at [Williams College](https://www.williams.edu/), I worked as a business analyst at a language-learning startup, a community marketer at a values-driven solar company in Vermont, and an independent SAT tutor.

Away from work, you might find me tinkering with my <Accent icon="coffee">espresso setup</Accent>, lost in a good book, or spelunking down a Wikipedia rabbit hole. I love journaling and collecting stationery, trying my hand at photography around San Francisco, and cooking for friends and family—with a cozy game or some quality cat time in between.

This is my little home on the internet. A place for [words](/blog/), <HomeLink href="/garden/" icon="flower">growing things</HomeLink>, and whatever comes next. Make yourself at home.
